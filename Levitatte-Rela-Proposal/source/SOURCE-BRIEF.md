LEVITATTE LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Transforming Hospital Teams. Elevating Every Patient Experience.

End-to-End Learning and Development Solutions for Healthcare Organisations

Every patient interaction shapes the reputation of a hospital.

From the first telephone call and registration experience to treatment coordination, billing and
discharge, patients and their families expect clarity, compassion, professionalism and confidence.

Levitatte Learning and Development partners with hospitals to build capable, compassionate and
service-driven teams through structured, practical and measurable learning programmes.

---

WHAT WE WILL HANDLE

Training Needs Analysis
We begin by understanding the hospital's people, processes and service expectations.
Training requirements are identified through management discussions, departmental inputs, employee
observations, patient feedback, complaints, audit findings and performance gaps.
This enables us to create training programmes that address the hospital's actual operational and
behavioural needs.

Competency and Role-Based Learning
Different hospital roles require different capabilities.
- Front-office and reception teams
- Patient relations teams
- Billing and administrative teams
- Nursing and clinical-support personnel
- Housekeeping and facility teams
- Security personnel
- Supervisors and department heads
- Managers and hospital leadership
Each programme is aligned with the employee's responsibilities and level of patient interaction.

---

NEW-EMPLOYEE INDUCTION
We help new employees understand:
- The hospital's vision, mission and values
- Workplace culture and expected conduct
- Patient rights and confidentiality
- Professional appearance and etiquette
- Departmental responsibilities
- Service and communication standards
- Safety and emergency awareness
- Internal escalation and reporting procedures
A structured induction programme helps employees become confident, connected and service-ready from
the beginning.

PATIENT EXPERIENCE AND SERVICE EXCELLENCE
Key areas include:
- Creating a positive first impression
- Welcoming and guiding patients
- Communicating with empathy
- Understanding patient and attendant concerns
- Managing expectations and delays
- Handling emotional or anxious individuals
- Communicating difficult information sensitively
- Complaint handling and service recovery
- Protecting patient dignity and privacy
- Creating consistent service standards
Our objective is to ensure that every employee understands their role in the patient experience.

COMMUNICATION AND BEHAVIOURAL DEVELOPMENT
Hospital employees frequently work in high-pressure and emotionally sensitive situations.
Our behavioural programmes cover:
- Verbal and non-verbal communication
- Active listening
- Telephone etiquette
- Professional workplace conduct
- Emotional intelligence
- Conflict management
- Stress management
- Problem-solving
- Personal accountability
- Time and priority management
- Cultural sensitivity
- Teamwork and collaboration
Employees learn how to communicate clearly, calmly and respectfully, even during challenging situations.

OUR LEARNING METHODOLOGY
Our programmes are practical, participative and connected to real hospital situations.
Training may include:
- Instructor-led workshops
- Role plays
- Hospital-based case studies
- Patient-interaction simulations
- Group activities
- Scenario discussions
- Microlearning sessions
- On-the-job observation
- Coaching and reinforcement
- Pre- and post-training assessments
Employees are not simply told what to do, they practise how to do it.

---

OUR FIVE-STAGE APPROACH
01. Diagnose - Understand the hospital's objectives, service challenges, employee capabilities and
    training priorities.
02. Design - Create customised programmes aligned with hospital policies, SOPs, employee roles and
    patient-experience standards.
03. Deliver - Conduct engaging, practical and role-specific learning interventions.
04. Reinforce - Support learning through refresher programmes, coaching, observations, job aids and
    corrective interventions.
05. Measure - Evaluate participation, knowledge improvement, behavioural change and organisational impact.
