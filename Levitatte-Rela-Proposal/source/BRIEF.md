### WHY THIS ORDER (the pedagogical argument — must be articulated in the doc)
Sequence logic: **Awareness -> Self -> Tools -> Inner capability -> Application -> Complexity ->
Sustainability -> Multiplication.** Three placements are deliberate and must be explained:
1. **Professional presence (M2) early** — the patient forms a judgement before a word is spoken; it is
   also the fastest visible win, which builds credibility for the whole programme.
2. **Difficult situations (M7) late, not early** — de-escalation and service recovery depend on active
   listening (M3), emotional regulation (M4) and agreed service standards (M5/M6) already being in
   place. Teaching complaint handling first is the most common design error and it does not hold.
   Personal resilience (M8) then follows immediately, because M7 is what surfaces the strain.
3. **Supervisors (M10) after the frontline, and Train-the-Trainer (M11) last** — supervisors must know
   the standard their teams were taught before being asked to coach and enforce it, and internal
   trainers must have completed the curriculum themselves before they can carry it. Trained staff
   returning to untrained supervisors regress; this ordering is what prevents that.

### Parallel standing track (NOT a sequential phase)
**New-Employee Induction** is built once during the diagnose/design stage and then delivered to each
new-joiner cohort as they arrive. It is a recurring track, not a week in the calendar. Content: vision/
mission/values, workplace culture & conduct, patient rights & confidentiality, professional appearance
& etiquette, departmental responsibilities, service & communication standards, safety & emergency
awareness, internal escalation & reporting.
